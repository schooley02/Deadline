## Enemies (Tasks & Habits)

Once an item goes overdue the tier class is dropped — `enemy-at-base`'s existing red pulse (border + box-shadow) owns that state instead, so the two visuals never compete. Deliberately NO new glow/color — the streak fire effect (session 59) already owns glow, so urgency here is communicated by motion only. Gated by the same `fx-off`/`fx-reduced`/`prefers-reduced-motion` settings as the streak effect. **Stage 2 (unscheduled, blocked on real animated sprites — currently one static PNG per category):** swap the bob keyframes for real 4-frame `steps()` walk-cycle sprite sheets; the tier/speed wiring carries over unchanged. See DECISIONS.md session 60, ROADMAP.md.

Fractions are tunable layout constants, not economy balance. **SUPERSEDED for landscape/desktop 2026-10-13:** top-level items now stand in 3 deterministic ground lanes dealt in agenda list order (`Layout.applyGroundLanes`, `CONFIG.GROUND_LANE_FEET_TOP_FRAC`/`BOTTOM_FRAC`); the random band is only the pre-first-tick placement.

## Defeating Enemies

- Defeat = user marks the task/habit complete (Attack button then tap enemy, or Complete/Defeat in the agenda list).
- On defeat: explosion animation, player earns points/currency + XP; the routine it belongs to earns XP.
- UNDO required: accidental completes must be reversible — keep a "recently defeated" view to restore from.

## Base

- A country church overlooking an eerie graveyard. 100 HP. **Healing: GRADUAL REGEN (decided 2026-07-18 with Jeremy on Fable; BUILT 2026-07-18, session 17 — [P2-GAME-012]):** the base heals 1 HP per 5 min (`CONFIG.BASE_REGEN_HP` / `CONFIG.BASE_REGEN_INTERVAL_MS`, balance-protocol tunable) continuously while the run is alive (`js/loop.js`'s `updateActiveItems`), clamped at 100. **Offline cap CONFIRMED 12 HP/item lifetime and partial countdown kept across reloads (2026-10-13, Jeremy; DECISIONS 2026-10-13).** **PAUSED while ANY threatening item is overdue (changed 2026-10-13, Jeremy):** regen and a single overdue item's damage were both 1 HP / 5 min, so one overdue item netted zero and the base never visibly dropped. Now the regen clock just stays planted at "now" while something is overdue (nothing banks), and regen resumes one full interval after the last overdue item clears. Negative-habit lurkers never count (they are never overdue). The same rule applies to offline/gap catch-up, but only for the time a zombie was actually attacking (changed 2026-10-26, Jeremy): `Damage.applyElapsedRegen` heals the QUIET part of the away span -- from the start of the span to the earliest moment any still-overdue threatening item began attacking (`max(its due time, span start)`; nothing can be completed while away) -- via the pure `Damage.computeQuietMs`. Offline time heals at the same rate, applied on restore BEFORE offline overdue damage is back-charged, since the quiet time came first (changed 2026-10-26: the clamp at 100 and any KO check now see the healing first) (`js/damage.js`'s `applyElapsedRegen`, wired into both `runOfflineCatchUp` and `runLiveGapCatchUp`) — net effect: a quiet base recovers overnight; a neglected one doesn't. Repair kits (ECONOMY.md's three tiers, unbuilt) are instant mid-run heals — the "I need HP now" lever on top of slow free regen. ## Base Health panel -- the numbers behind it (BUILT 2026-10-26, `docs/BASE_PANEL_PLAN.md`)

Display only: the panel and the HUD health chip introduce NO new rules. Every number comes from the pure `js/baseModel.js` (`BaseModel.build(ctx)`, Jest-tested in `test/baseModel.test.js`), fed from CONFIG constants and live state by script.js `baseHealthContext()`. Layout and controls: UI_UX.md "Base Health panel".

- **State.** `attacked` when at least one attacker exists and HP > 0; otherwise `full` at max HP, else `healing`. An **attacker** is an overdue item that is not `Items.isNonThreatening` (KO ghosts and negative-habit lurkers never count), the same set that pauses regen in the live loop. HP color bands (also the HUD ring): > 75 full, > 50 moderate, > 25 low, otherwise critical.
- **Church art stages.** The art changes at the floors 25 / 50 / 75 (`BaseModel.STAGE_FLOORS`; matches the five damage-state images). While attacked, the art worsens once HP drops to the highest floor strictly below current HP (`worsenAtHp` = floor + 1 is the last HP of the better stage). While healing, HP floor + 1 repairs it one stage (`nextStageHp`, `healsToStage`).
- **Damage timers (attacked).** Each attacker hits for `OVERDUE_DAMAGE` every `tickMs` from its `lastDamageTickTime`; `tickMs` is `DAMAGE_INTERVAL_MS` unless a running work timer stretches it (`Timer.damageIntervalMs`), which is why the chip's "-N / 5m" can be fractional. A hit already due is treated as about to fire (next hit = now). The panel simulates the merged hit schedule of all attackers to get the next-hit countdown, HP lost in the next 15 min (the striped bar segment, capped at current HP), time until the art worsens, and the **"church falls in" ETA** (time until cumulative damage >= current HP). The ETA deliberately ignores regen, because regen is paused while anything is attacking, and it ignores completions and kits (it is "at this rate").
- **Healing timers (healing).** "+1 HP in" = time to the next regen tick (`lastRegenTickMs + BASE_REGEN_INTERVAL_MS`, floored at now); "Full in" = next tick + (ceil(missing / `BASE_REGEN_HP`) - 1) intervals; the stage timer is the same arithmetic to the next art stage. Regen keeps healing offline and only pauses for the span a zombie was actually attacking (see Base above).
- **Repair kits.** Effective heal = min(kit heal, HP missing); the rest is wasted (Large at 40 HP wastes 15). The kit info popover (`basePanel.js kitNotes`) lists wasted HP and a "lands 1 HP short" note when a kit would land exactly on a stage floor (25 / 50 / 75), one HP short of the better church look. Kits are usable only with one held and 0 < HP < max (0 HP is game over; nothing revives). Value shown: while healing, time saved = effective HP x regen interval; while attacked, time bought = effective HP x average ms-per-HP of the current attackers. Using a kit goes through the normal `handleShopUse` path, so inventory and run stats behave exactly as from the shop.
- **Pushback in the panel.** The attackers card offers only the cheapest tier (`pushback_1hr`, level-discounted price from `Progression.shopDiscount`), up to 3 attackers, most overdue first. It runs the shipped `handlePushback` path, so the from-now rule applies (a pushback counts from max(now, due); DECISIONS 2026-10-26). Disabled with "Need N more pts" when unaffordable.
- **Not modeled.** The panel does not forecast completions, and during a time-slider preview the chip is hidden (the projected health from `TimeSlider.projectBaseHealth` is conservative: no regen if an item is due by the preview time). Balance flag carried over: a 50-pt pushback costs about the same per HP as a repair kit; revisit with real play data via the balance-tuning process (no numbers changed here).

## Overdue Damage

- When an enemy reaches the base (item is due/overdue): base takes 1 HP damage every 5 minutes until the item is completed.
- If still incomplete after 1 hour, the enemy gets INSIDE the base and starts damaging the health of the Routine it belongs to.
- **Live damage is uncapped** — an item you leave overdue while actually watching the game keeps costing 1 HP per `DAMAGE_INTERVAL_MS`, indefinitely. The per-item cap below applies ONLY to time the player was away.
- **Items created already overdue start their damage clock at spawn (fixed 2026-07-18).** Adding a task whose deadline has already passed costs 0 HP up front — it spawns at the base and begins ticking from now. Previously the clock was parked at the due time and the live loop replayed the gap, so a task backdated 3 hours instantly cost 36 HP.
- **Suspended game loop = time away (fixed 2026-07-18).** If the loop stops ticking for ≥ `CONFIG.LIVE_GAP_THRESHOLD_MS` (30s) — laptop sleep, throttled background tab — `runLiveGapCatchUp()` charges the gap through the same capped path a reload uses, sharing one `OFFLINE_DAMAGE_CAP_PER_ITEM` lifetime budget per item (see Offline Catch-up below), then resumes normal live ticking. Previously nothing guarded this path (the cap only covered reloads) and the live loop replayed the whole gap at one damage interval per 50ms tick — an overnight sleep flattened a full base in seconds. Pending damage is tracked via `lastDamageTickTime` in whole intervals (remainder preserved), so a background tab waking once a minute can't round its way out of damage.
- **Editing a due date INTO THE PAST never back-charges (fixed 2026-07-21).** `Items.recomputeOverdueStateAfterEdit`'s pulled-into-the-past branch starts the damage clock at the edit-landing moment (`item.lastDamageTickTime = now`), not the due time — an edit that pulls a deadline hours or days into the past costs 0 HP up front, matching the created-already-overdue precedent above. This is a deliberate design choice (Jeremy, Fable, 2026-07-20 visual-audit session): an edit is a bookkeeping correction, not evidence of an attack the player dodged, so nothing is owed for time that already passed before the edit. Live damage from that point forward stays UNBOUNDED as always — only the retroactive back-charge is removed, not ongoing pressure. (Undoing a completion via `uncompleteItem` on an item whose due date is in the past has the same latent hazard and is NOT yet fixed — open design question, see ROADMAP.md Known bugs.)

## Day Rollover (day-advance mechanism, BUILT 2026-07-19 session 32)

`currentGameDate` is the game's notion of "which day it is." It's set once at initGame() (midnight today) and restored from the save. Before this session nothing ever advanced it, so a session that spanned midnight (close overnight, reopen next day) left the game stuck on the old day — the daily generators ran with a stale date and today's habit/routine-task instances never spawned. Base HP / offline damage / regen / days-survived all derive from REAL elapsed time and were never affected; only the game-DAY concept was broken.

On restore (`js/state.js`'s restoreGameState, after re-adding saved items and before spawning today's), if real time has crossed into a later calendar day than the save's `currentGameDate` (`DayRollover.hasDayRolledOver`, `js/dayRollover.js`), the prior day's RECURRING instances are closed out, then `currentGameDate` advances to today so the generators spawn today's. Recurring instances = those carrying a `definitionId` (habits + routine tasks); one-off tasks and sub-tasks have none and are left alone — a missed one-off deadline stays an overdue base threat, it isn't a per-day concept.

**Settlement per item is now a THREE-way fork** (sub-session 4 added the check-in split 2026-07-19; sub-session 5 added Cheat Day 2026-07-19 — see "Daily Check-in" and "Cheat Day Token" below). For a negative-habit lurker, `state.js` checks, in order: (1) does an active Cheat Day cover this lurker's day (`deps.isCheatDayExcusedForItem`) → `Items.settleExcusedCheatDay`, checked FIRST, ahead of everything else; (2) is this the SINGLE most recent prior day (`DayRollover.isFromPreviousDay`) → `Items.markPendingCheckIn` (deferred to the check-in prompt); (3) otherwise → the existing `Items.settleStaleRecurringInstance`:

- **Negative-habit lurker from an OLDER day → auto-resolved as AVOIDED** (session-26's generous "prior days default to avoided"): the full avoid reward — success occurrence keyed to the instance's original date, streak++, XP + rate-multiplied points. Because the occurrence is keyed to that day (not today), today still spawns a fresh lurker (the temptation returns daily). Not surfaced in "Completed Today" (it happened in the past; surfacing IS the check-in's job for the previous day specifically).
- **Positive habit → dropped.** Its miss was already recorded by markAsOverdue when the instance was re-added on restore (streak reset, rate-history miss); the stale instance is then removed so it doesn't linger into today.
- **Routine task → dropped** (Jeremy's call, session 32: daily routine tasks are per-day like habits, not persistent deadlines).

A closed-out recurring instance charges NO offline base damage (removed before runOfflineCatchUp): recurring-habit consequences are behavioral (points/rate), base HP is for one-off deadline failures. Multi-day gaps settle only what's on the board — the intervening days never generated instances, so no retroactive misses are fabricated (matches the offline "punish count, not duration" philosophy).

**LIVE mid-session rollover (BUILT 2026-07-20, Cowork session).** The settle-and-advance fork above was originally restore-path-only; a tab left running past midnight just stayed stale until the next reload. `js/state.js`'s `performDayRollover(deps, now)` extracts that fork out of `restoreGameState` so both paths share one implementation — `restoreGameState` calls it on load (unchanged behavior), and a new `State.checkLiveDayRollover(deps)` calls it every tick from `js/loop.js`'s `updateGame` (optional collaborator, same "omitted = no-op" tolerance as `checkDayPagerRollover`), so a session left open across midnight settles yesterday's stale recurring instances and spawns today's live, with an immediate `saveGame()` rather than waiting for the 5s autosave window. No offline-catch-up animation runs on the live path (nothing was offline — the tab was live the whole time). See DECISIONS.md 2026-07-20.

## Daily Check-in (BUILT 2026-07-19, sub-session 4, [P1-DATA-005])

The check-in SURFACE for the previous day's negative-habit lurker (frozen routine slots — a separate later ticket — are NOT part of this). `Items.markPendingCheckIn` (called instead of `settleStaleRecurringInstance` for the previous day only, per the fork above) records `habitDef.pendingCheckIn = { originalDueDate }` — additive, no schema bump, absent on every pre-existing habit/save — and removes the lurker so today's fresh one spawns without a duplicate. On the next page load, `js/ui/checkIn.js`'s `showCheckInModal` renders one card per pending habit ("Did you successfully avoid [name] yesterday?") with **Successfully avoided** / **I indulged** buttons, resolved by `Items.resolvePendingCheckIn(habitDefId, outcome, deps)`:

- **avoided** → `Habits.applyHabitCompletion` keyed to the marker's `originalDueDate`: success occurrence, streak++, XP + rate-multiplied points (identical economics to the old silent auto-resolve, just player-confirmed).
- **indulged** → `Habits.applyHabitIndulgence`: miss occurrence, streak zeroed, non-clamping points debit via `Economy.applyIndulgenceCost` (same debt path as the live "I indulged" button).

Also: **"I'll check this later" snooze** (spec PROJECT_SPEC.md ~646) — a plain in-session `setTimeout` (`CONFIG.CHECK_IN_SNOOZE_MS`, 4 hours) that re-shows the modal if anything's still pending. NOT persisted across reload — closing the tab and reopening before 4 hours just re-prompts immediately on the next restore. This ticket built the check-in surface only, not a scheduling system.

## Cheat Day Token (BUILT 2026-07-19, sub-session 5, [P1-DATA-005])

A shop consumable (200 pts, held-inventory exponential pricing like repair kits — see ECONOMY.md) that excuses one negative habit's indulgence for one specific day: while active, indulging costs nothing and records NO occurrence — the day is EXCUSED, not a success or a miss; streak is preserved, not incremented (decided session 26, Fable).

**Targeting:** unlike repair kits (a shop-card "Use" button) or pushback (applies to whatever enemy you tap), Cheat Day is Buy-to-hold like a repair kit but "used" by tapping a specific negative-habit lurker's popup — a new **"Use Cheat Day (N held)"** button (`js/ui/popups.js`, shown only when held > 0) consumes one token (`Shop.consume`) and sets that habit definition's `habitDef.cheatDayDate` to the lurker's occurrence date (`Habits.toOccurrenceDate`). The popup rebuilds itself (deferred via `setTimeout(0)` — replacing the button with an "active" note mid-click is the same event-bubbling hazard that bit the shop's Buy/Use buttons in session 21) to show "🎟️ Cheat Day active — indulging today is free."

**Schema:** `habitDef.cheatDayDate` (a `'YYYY-MM-DD'` string or `null`) — schemaVersion 4→5 migration, additive, seeded `null` on every pre-existing habit.

**Where it's checked** (`Items.isCheatDayExcused(habitDef, originalDueDate)` — true when `cheatDayDate` matches that day's occurrence date):
- **Live indulge** (`Items.indulgeHabit`): skips the points debit and streak/occurrence changes entirely. **The marker is KEPT through the rest of the day (changed 2026-07-19 session 57):** since an excused indulge records no occurrence, the marker is the only state telling the spawn dedupe the day is resolved — nulling it (the original session-34 behavior) let a same-day reload respawn the lurker with its cheat cover gone. The marker is date-scoped so it self-expires the next calendar day; "one use per token" is enforced structurally by the spawn gate (at most one instance per day = at most one excused indulge per token).
- **Spawn dedupe** (`Habits.selectHabitDefsToSpawn`, session 57): a `cheatDayDate` matching the spawn day blocks a fresh instance, parallel to `skipDayDate`.
- **Check-in resolution** (`Items.resolvePendingCheckIn`'s `'indulged'` branch): same excused check, defensive — in practice a cheat-day-covered day never reaches the check-in card at all (see the rollover fork above), but this guards a same-day edge case.
- **Rollover** (`state.js`): `Items.settleExcusedCheatDay` — checked FIRST, ahead of the check-in-eligible and auto-avoid paths; clears the marker and removes the lurker with no points/xp/streak/occurrence change, silently, matching NEGATIVE_HABITS_PLAN.md's "the check-in card for that day auto-resolves as excused."

## Habits: Positive & Negative

- Positive habit completed, or negative habit avoided → earn points.
- Negative habit indulged, or positive habit missed → lose points. Points CAN go negative.
- **Negative-habit model (decided 2026-07-19 session 26, Fable — see DECISIONS.md; implementation is [P1-DATA-005] sub-sessions 2–5, in progress):** the "A2 lurker" model. A negative habit spawns a zombie that **lurks near the fence and never advances — no base damage, no overdue path** (base HP is for deadline failures only; a lapse is invisible to the game and can only be self-reported). Tap the lurker → "I indulged" → immediate points loss (via the rate multiplier, same math as awards), streak zeroed. **An indulged lurker stays gone for the rest of that day, including across reloads (fixed 2026-07-19 session 56):** the spawn dedupe (`Habits.selectHabitDefsToSpawn`) treats any recorded occurrence for the day — completed / overdue / indulged — as "day resolved" and won't spawn a fresh instance (indulging deliberately never sets `lastCompletionDate`, so the older completion-based dedupe alone missed this). The cheat-day-excused variant (which records no occurrence by design) is covered separately: the kept `cheatDayDate` marker gates the respawn — fixed 2026-07-19 session 57, see the Cheat Day Token section. **Same-day prompt (2026-10-21, Jeremy):** while the game is open, the moment a negative habit's time arrives a "Time's up" popup asks Avoided / I indulged / Later (once per habit per day, `habitDef.arrivalPromptedFor`; Later leaves it to the next-morning check-in; `CheckIn.queueArrivalPrompt`, loop hook `onLurkerArrived`). Undecided days resolve at the **next morning's check-in** (binary avoided/indulged cards per PROJECT_SPEC); days older than the previous day default to avoided. Debt is **unbounded and orthogonal to base health** — its function is keeping indulgence costly at 0 balance, not punishment; the HUD shows negative balances in red with an agency-framed break-even nudge. The escalating consequence for sustained (3+ day) indulgence is frozen routine slots — a separate later ticket. **POSITION UPDATE 2026-10-13 (Jeremy):** a lurker is placed on the normal timeline by its tempting time (walk animation by urgency) and parks idle beside the base once the time passes (`lurker-parked`); it is still never overdue and never damages. This supersedes any "fixed far-right post" wording above (DECISIONS 2026-10-13).
- Time-of-day matters: track when habits are due/completed to surface behavior patterns (help break negative patterns, reinforce hard positive ones).
- Streaks: missing a habit (overdue) resets its streak to 0. **The streak is a VISUAL/legibility layer only (built 2026-07-18, session 16): agenda badge + on-fire sprite at high streak — no economy effect.** The points bonus instead comes from a rolling **success rate**: over the habit's last `CONFIG.HABIT_RATE_WINDOW` (22, re-tuned 2026-07-21 from 14 — "~22 reps to form a habit") recorded occurrences (`occurrenceHistory` on the habit definition — see DATA_SCHEMA.md), success = completed / miss = overdue. Rate 100% → 3× points; ≥80% → 2×; else 1× (`CONFIG.HABIT_RATE_TIERS`; balance-protocol tunable; capped at 3× by construction; was ≥90% 2× / ≥70% 1.5× "task parity" before the 2026-07-21 re-anchor to a 1-pt base, see DECISIONS.md). Multiplier is 1× until `CONFIG.HABIT_RATE_MIN_SAMPLE` (22 — deliberately the full window) occurrences are recorded, so a new habit can't instantly max out. (Doc corrected 2026-10-25; the numbers themselves changed 2026-07-21.) Applies to POINTS ONLY, never XP — the multiplier prices into the Milestone 3 shop without accelerating levels. Occurrences are recorded on completion (success), on going overdue (miss), and a late same-day completion upserts over that day's earlier miss. Uncompleting recomputes the multiplier from the still-current history then pops today's entry, so refunds are symmetric by construction (this structurally dissolved the old flat-+5 refund-asymmetry bug — it can't recur). Polarity is routed through one `Habits.occurrenceSuccess(isNegative, event)` seam; completed/overdue map completed→success/overdue→miss for both polarities. **[P1-DATA-005] session 25 (2026-07-19) added the 'indulged' event to this seam plus a pure `Habits.applyHabitIndulgence(...)` applier** — the explicit "I lapsed on this negative habit today" action, which records a miss and computes points lost the same way `applyHabitCompletion` computes points gained (no-op for positive habits). **Pure core only — not yet wired to `items.js`/UI/economy**, so nothing in-game changes yet; see `docs/NEGATIVE_HABITS_PLAN.md` for the remaining sub-sessions (inverted negative-overdue semantics, negative balance, daily check-in, day-tokens), gated on a Fable fork session. Replaced the old deterministic flat +5 at streak ≥3. See DECISIONS.md 2026-07-18 (session 16) and session 25.
- **Editing a live habit instance** (agenda row edit pencil, implemented 2026-07-17): opens the habit-definition editor (name/category/frequency/time-of-day/type), pre-filled from `definedHabits`. Saving updates the definition AND syncs any already-spawned instance's name/category/isNegative + re-renders its agenda row immediately. **Time-of-day changes DO re-time today's instance (2026-10-21, Jeremy):** the due time is re-derived on the instance's own day (`Habits.retimeHabitInstance`) and overdue state re-evaluated (`Items.recomputeOverdueStateAfterEdit`), so moving a habit to a bucket that has already passed makes today's zombie attack from the moment you save — NO back-charge and NO missed occurrence / streak reset (the edit is a correction; the natural overdue transition still records the miss). Moving it later un-overdues it (a miss already recorded stays recorded). Frequency/schedule changes still only take effect from the next day's instance. ## Sub-tasks

- Sub-tasks render as their own smaller (`CONFIG.SUBTASK_ENEMY_WIDTH` = 96×96 (was 64×64 until 2026-10-13)) enemy sprites, bottom-aligned with their parent (feet on the same ground line — not top-aligned) and fan out to the LEFT of it, toward the church (CHANGED 2026-10-13, Jeremy; was alternating right/left): 1st just left of the parent, each further sub chaining further left in creation order, clamped at the base edge. Desktop and mobile landscape; portrait uses the strip rules (docs/UI_UX.md). Sub-tasks also draw IN FRONT of their parent (z + 1).
- **Offsets are computed from the sprites' VISIBLE graphic edges, not their box edges.** The zombie PNGs have large transparent margins (visible graphic is ~44%–92% of the box width depending on category, and not centered — e.g. lifestyle leans right). Per-category margins were measured from the PNGs' alpha channels on 2026-07-17 and live in `CONFIG.ZOMBIE_VISIBLE_MARGINS`; `getSubTaskClusterOffset` chains each sibling's visible edge `CONFIG.SUBTASK_CLUSTER_GAP_PX` (8px) from the previous one's, handling mixed sibling categories. If sprite art is ever redrawn, re-measure and update the margin table (alpha-bbox script in DECISIONS.md 2026-07-17 entry). The parent sprite always renders above any overlapping sub-task sprite (z-index: parent 10, sub-task 5).
- Sub-tasks track the PARENT's timeline position (plus the cluster offset above) rather than their own due date, so a sub-task due only a little earlier/later than its parent stays visually clustered with it instead of drifting away. A sub-task only breaks from the cluster and shows further ahead (closer to the base) on its own timeline if its own due date is due *significantly* earlier than the parent's (currently: its own timeline x would be 150px+ closer to the base — `CONFIG.SUBTASK_AHEAD_THRESHOLD_PX`).
- Sub-tasks do NOT get their own entry in the main agenda list — only nested inside their parent's list item.
- **Growing/shrinking parent visuals — BUILT** (2026-07-19, session 50, SUBTASKS_PLAN.md sub-session 4; resolved-fork design from session 46). The parent's rendered box grows `CONFIG.PARENT_GROWTH_PER_SUB` (15%) per OPEN sub-task, capped at `CONFIG.PARENT_GROWTH_MAX_SUBS` (4 subs → +60% at the cap), and shrinks back automatically as each sub completes/is removed (`Movement.getParentGrowthScale`/`getParentRenderWidth`, derived fresh every tick in `Loop.updateActiveItems` from the live `item.subTasks` array — no new hooks needed at the completion/cascade/refund call sites). The background sprite scales WITH the box (`.enemy.parent-scaled` in enemySprites.css, applied only to top-level tasks) rather than staying fixed at 128px, so `getSubTaskClusterOffset`'s visible-edge math stays accurate at any size and the fan never detaches. Height is untouched (only width grows), so bottom-alignment ("feet on the ground line") needs no changes. Live-verified in Chrome: 3 subs grew the parent with an attached fan at every step; completing them one at a time shrank it back to exactly 128px with zero drift.
- **A parent with open sub-tasks cannot be completed** (BUILT 2026-07-19, session 47, sub-session 1) — the checkbox is disabled ("N sub-tasks remaining") in both the agenda row and the popup, and `Items.completeItem` refuses at the source with a result object if somehow bypassed. Before this, completing (or deleting) a parent with open subs stranded the children as agenda-invisible, base-damaging zombies (nested-only rendering means no row exists without a live parent to nest under) — found via a live playtest, see DECISIONS.md session 46/47. `Items.removeItem` now cascades in both directions: deleting a parent sweeps every child, deleting a sub syncs the parent's counters without marking it completed. A restore-time sanitizer (`State.sanitizeOrphanedSubTasks`) heals any pre-existing orphan in an old save by promoting it to a standalone top-level task.
- **Sub-task due dates are dependent on the parent's** (BUILT 2026-07-19, session 48 — SUBTASKS_PLAN.md sub-session 2): default and maximum = the parent's deadline, earlier is allowed. The creation and edit forms cap the date input (`max` attr) and loudly REJECT a later value on save (no silent clamping on manual entry); `Items.createTaskItemData` silently clamps as a data-layer backstop for programmatic paths. Pulling the parent's deadline earlier re-clamps any now-later children down to it (`Items.clampSubTasksToParentDeadline`, routed through `recomputeOverdueStateAfterEdit` per child so overdue state/positions stay correct — a clamp into the past marks the child overdue); pushing it later leaves children alone (NOT delta-shift). Pushback purchases on a sub-task clamp to the parent's deadline too (pure `Items.clampedSubTaskDueDate`).
- **Sub-task economy** (BUILT 2026-07-19, session 49 — SUBTASKS_PLAN.md sub-session 3; points rule RE-DESIGNED 2026-07-21, Jeremy): a sub-task pays `CONFIG.SUBTASK_XP`/`SUBTASK_POINTS` (5 XP / 1 pt). A parent that ever had a completed sub-task (`completedSubTasks > 0`) pays **0 points** on its own completion but still its full XP (10); a task that never had subs pays its normal base. The high-priority ×2 rule applies to a sub's own priority flag on top of its base (max 2 pts, XP never multiplies) — see ECONOMY.md. ## Offline Catch-up (implemented 2026-07-17)

- On restore, zombies animate (ease-out, capped at `CONFIG.OFFLINE_CATCHUP_MAX_MS` = 5s) from their last-saved positions to their current-time positions. Skipped for very brief absences (`CONFIG.OFFLINE_ANIMATION_THRESHOLD_MS` = 30s) — positions just update instantly.
- **Offline overdue damage policy** (decided with Jeremy, see DECISIONS.md 2026-07-17): each item that spent offline time overdue is back-charged its real elapsed damage (same 1 HP/`DAMAGE_INTERVAL_MS` rate as live play), but capped at `CONFIG.OFFLINE_DAMAGE_CAP_PER_ITEM` = 12 HP **for that item's entire lifetime**, not per restore. Design principle: punish the COUNT of neglected items, not the DURATION you were away — sleeping/being busy shouldn't be able to kill the base by itself, but enough neglected items still can (8+ at the current cap). Time already charged live (before the item was saved) is never double-charged; the chargeable window starts at whichever is later, the item's due time or the start of the offline period. Offline progression itself is capped at 3 days (`CONFIG.OFFLINE_MAX_MS`, per PROJECT_SPEC).
- **Editing a task's due date re-evaluates overdue state** (`recomputeOverdueStateAfterEdit`, companion fix to the above): pushing an overdue task's deadline into the future clears its overdue flag/visuals and stops further damage — the intended escape hatch if a deadline was set too aggressively. Pulling a not-yet-due task's deadline into the past marks it overdue immediately. Not yet wired into the habit-instance editor (frequency/time-of-day changes there still don't retroactively move today's instance — pre-existing gap, see ROUTINES.md/DECISIONS.md).
- **Offline HEALING (gradual recovery while away) — BUILT 2026-07-18, session 17 [P2-GAME-012]** (see Base above): same 1 HP/5min rate applied on restore BEFORE offline damage (reordered 2026-10-26), clamped at 100, and only for the QUIET part of the window: the time before the earliest still-overdue threatening item began attacking (`computeQuietMs`, 2026-10-26). Uses `computeRegenTicks(quietMs, CONFIG.BASE_REGEN_INTERVAL_MS)` — whole intervals only, remainder discarded at that point (the live loop's regen clock resets to "now" right after, rather than carrying the remainder forward). A suspended-but-not-reloaded loop (laptop sleep, throttled tab) gets the same treatment via `runLiveGapCatchUp`, using the gap since the live loop's own last regen tick.

## KO ghosts BUILT 2026-10-25 (Build 1); rescheduling + timers decided, NOT built (Fable; `docs/DESIGN_QUESTIONS_KO_EDIT_TIMER.md`, DECISIONS 2026-10-25)

Misses already recorded at KO stand; the item that was overdue at KO simply stops being overdue. Rollover: a ghost positive habit / routine task is removed with no miss; a ghost negative habit skips the check-in card and auto-settles (0 points). Manual deactivation and frozen slots still recall items (pure removal).
